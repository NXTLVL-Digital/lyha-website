Section 01

Is this right for my child?

Three quick checks. If you can answer yes to all three, Mites is the right starting line.

Age

6 to 8 years old

Mites covers ages six through eight at the start of the season. If your child is older, look at Squirts.

Skating

Can skate unassisted

Your child needs to be able to move on the ice without holding the wall. They don't need to be polished. They need to be on their feet.

If they're brand new to skating, we recommend Learn to Play first. It teaches the fundamentals before Mites picks up the puck.

Background

Most come from Learn to Play

A few children come in with rec skating experience. The rest start with Learn to Play and graduate into Mites the following season.

Section 02

What's the commitment?

Mites is a development division. No regular game schedule. A short jamboree calendar. Practice-heavy by design.

Season
September through February
Practices
2 per week at LaHaye
Games
0 (development focus)
Jamborees
About 3 per season
Travel
Mostly within 90 min

A jamboree is a half-day of hockey games at another rink. Typically 9 AM to 1 PM, at rinks one to three hours from Lynchburg. Mites do about three of these a season. They're the closest thing to "games" in this division, and they're the most travel a Mites family is asked to take on.

Where to get gear.

Section 07

Mites questions.

Specific to this division. The general FAQ covers broader hockey questions.

My child has never skated. Should we sign up for Mites?

Start with Learn to Play. Mites assumes children can already skate unassisted. Learn to Play is one season focused on building those fundamentals. Most LYHA players take that path before joining Mites.

Does my child need their own gear to start?

Yes, full equipment is required. We sometimes have donated used gear you can buy with a donation, but sizes and stock aren't guaranteed. Ask when you register before buying new. Play It Again Sports in Roanoke is good for used gear and Pure Hockey in Morrisville (just outside Raleigh) is the closest place for brand-new equipment.

What's a jamboree?

A half-day of hockey games hosted at another rink. Typically 9 AM to 1 PM. Mites travel to rinks one to three hours from Lynchburg, about three times per season. They're the closest thing to a "game day" in this division.

Are there tryouts?

No. Mites is open enrollment with a development focus. Every child who registers gets a spot.

Are games part of the season?

Not regular ones. Mites focuses on practice and skill development. Jamborees are the only competitive play, and the format is friendly. The goal is learning, not winning.
