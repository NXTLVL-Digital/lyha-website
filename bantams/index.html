Section 01

Is this right for my child?

Three things to think through before reaching out about Bantams.

Age

13 to 14 years old

Bantams covers ages thirteen and fourteen at the start of the season. Eligibility follows USA Hockey birth-year classifications. If you're unsure whether your child qualifies, ask us and we'll confirm.

Ages 11 to 12 belong in Peewee. Ages 14 to 15 belong in U16.

Tryouts

Competitive placement

Bantams has tryouts. We hold them in late summer. Contact us in the spring to get on the list. Kids coming from Peewee at LYHA or from programs at other clubs are both welcome to try out.

Coaches evaluate skating, puck skills, hockey sense, and coachability. We give families honest feedback regardless of outcome.

Commitment

More of everything

Two to three practices a week, 15 to 20 games per season, and overnight travel on a regular basis. This is a family commitment, not just a kid's commitment.

If the schedule is a concern, talk to us before tryouts. We'd rather you know the full picture going in.

Section 04

What it costs.

Cost should not be the reason a Lynchburg kid can't play.

Bantams tuition covers ice time, coaching, and USA Hockey and PVAHA registration. Travel costs, including gas and hotels for away weekends, are handled by families separately. Current season pricing is included in the registration link we send after tryouts. Financial aid is available. Ask any board member.

Section 07

Bantams questions.

Specific to this division. The general FAQ covers broader hockey questions.

How do tryouts work?

Tryouts are held at LaHaye in late summer, typically over two sessions. Coaches look at skating ability, puck skills, positional awareness, and how your child responds to coaching. We use jersey numbers so coaches can evaluate without knowing names. Results come out within a few days of the final session.

My child played Peewee at LYHA last season. Do they still have to try out?

Yes. Every player tries out each season regardless of history. Rosters are built fresh. Prior experience at LYHA is context coaches consider, but it doesn't guarantee a spot.

How many overnight trips should we expect?

A typical Bantams season includes three to five overnight trips. Some are single-night, some are tournament weekends with two nights away. We publish the full schedule before the season begins so families can plan ahead.

Can my child play a school sport and Bantams in the same season?

Some kids manage it, but the schedules can conflict. Two to three practices a week plus away game weekends is a real time commitment. Talk to your child's school coach and give us an honest picture of your availability. We'll tell you whether it's workable.

What happens if my child doesn't make the team?

We'll give you specific feedback about what coaches saw and what would help. For kids who are close but not quite ready, we'll suggest a path. We don't leave families without a clear answer.
