First Steps · Ages 4 to 8

New to hockey?
Start here.

Learn to Play is for children who have never been on ice, or who have skated but never played hockey. No experience needed. Most kids who play for LYHA today came through this program first.

Section 01

Is this right for my child?

Three quick checks. If you can answer yes to all three, Learn to Play is the right place to start.

Age

4 to 8 years old

Learn to Play is designed for this age range. If your child is 6 or older and has already done LTP, they may be ready for Mites. Reach out and we'll help you figure out the right fit.

Experience

Little to none

Your child has never played hockey, or has skated recreationally but never touched a puck. That's exactly who this program is for. Coaches start from the beginning.

Readiness

Curious, not committed

Your child doesn't need to know they want to play hockey. Curiosity is enough. Most kids figure out if it's their thing within the first few sessions. If it isn't, that's a fine outcome too.

Section 02

What your child will learn.

Sessions cover three core things. By the end of a Learn to Play season, most kids are ready to move on to Mites.

Skating

Getting comfortable on the ice

Balance, stopping, and moving without holding the wall. Children start from zero if needed. Coaches know how to get a nervous kid moving. This is the foundation everything else builds on.

Puck Skills

Stick handling and shooting

How a stick works. Moving a puck, shooting on a net, and passing to a teammate. Introduced at a pace that matches where each child is, not where a coach wants them to be.

The Game

How hockey works

Positions, the ice surface, and what's happening when play is moving. By the end of the season, children understand the game well enough to follow it and play a real role in it.

Section 08

Learn to Play questions.

Specific to this program. The general FAQ covers broader hockey questions.

My child has never been on ice. Is that OK?

Yes. Learn to Play starts from zero. Coaches are experienced with children who have never worn skates. Your child will not be the only one starting from the beginning.

My child is nervous. Is that normal?

Very normal. Most kids are nervous before their first session. Coaches know this and pace the first few sessions around getting comfortable, not getting fast. If your child is nervous, let the coach know before they step on the ice.

Does my child need their own gear?

Not for a first season. We have loaner gear available at no charge. When your child is ready for their own equipment, Play It Again Sports in Roanoke is a good first stop for used gear.

What happens after Learn to Play?

Most kids move to Mites after one Learn to Play season. Mites is the youngest travel division and assumes children can already skate and handle a puck. Learn to Play builds exactly those skills. A few kids repeat LTP for a second season if they want more time before the jump. That's fine. We'll help you decide at the end of the season.

My child already skates. Do they still need Learn to Play?

Not always. If your child can skate unassisted and is 6 or older, they may be ready to go straight to Mites. Contact us and we'll talk through it. We'd rather place them right than make them wait a season they don't need.

Is there a tryout or skill test?

No. Learn to Play is open enrollment. Every child who registers gets a spot.

Ready for More?

After Learn to Play comes Mites.

Most kids who finish Learn to Play move to Mites the following season. Mites is the youngest travel division at LYHA. Same rink, same coaches, a bigger game.
