# LYHA Brand Voice

This is the canonical reference for any copy that goes on the Lynchburg Youth Hockey Association website or any LYHA-branded communication. Anyone (or any AI) writing for LYHA reads this first.

## Audience

Most visitors to lynchburg.hockey are parents who don't know hockey. Lynchburg sits in a region where ice hockey is rare, and the people landing on our site are usually asking their first questions about a sport they've never followed.

Talk to them like a neighbor would. Don't assume hockey vocabulary. Don't oversell. Don't condescend.

## Voice

Direct. State facts. Avoid hedge phrases.

Plain. Short sentences. Conversational rhythm.

Honest. About cost. About commitment. About what's required. About what we don't know yet.

Parent-respecting. Their kid, their decision. Our job is to make the decision easier, not to push.

## Voice anti-patterns

Avoid marketing register: "thrilled to," "honored to," "passionate about," "delighted to."

Avoid aspirational generic: "unlock potential," "elevate your child," "lifelong friendships," "transformative experience."

Avoid over-warm closes: "we'd love to," "we can't wait to meet," "looking forward to seeing you on the ice."

Avoid empty parallels used to fill space: "From [X] to [Y]," "Whether [X] or [Y]," "Not just [X], but [Y]."

## AI-generated tells to avoid

These phrases and patterns have become widely recognized in 2025-2026 as signals of AI-written copy. Avoid them in LYHA content even when individually they seem fine. Sources at the end of this file.

### Punctuation

Em dashes (—). Replace with periods, commas, or rephrase. Real human writing uses em dashes rarely.

Excessive bolding mid-sentence. Use bold only for true emphasis, not as a formatting habit.

Curly quotes and smart apostrophes are fine. Don't worry about those.

### Buzzwords and metaphors

Avoid: delve, delving, tapestry, landscape (as metaphor), ecosystem (as metaphor), robust, world-class, unparalleled, transformative, seamless, holistic, leverage, navigate (when not literal), embark, harness, unleash, showcase (as verb), ensure, facilitate, vibrant, pivotal, intricate, elevate, empower, foster, cultivate, nurture, tap into, level up, next-level, best-in-class, right-sized.

### Sentence patterns

Avoid:

- "It's not about X, it's about Y" formula
- "In a world where..." openers
- "It's worth noting that..." and "It's important to note..."
- "This represents..." / "This emphasizes..." / "This indicates..." / "This reflects..." as opening verbs
- "Furthermore," "Moreover," "Additionally," as sentence openers
- "Ultimately," "In essence," "At the end of the day," as hedges
- Long lists of parallel adjectives ("the dynamic, vibrant, and innovative...")

### Closings to avoid

- "We'd love to..."
- "Don't hesitate to reach out"
- "We're here for you"
- "Looking forward to..."
- "Embark on..." anything
- "Begin your journey..." anything

## Word substitutions when speaking to parents

When the audience is parents (not coaches, not hockey-fluent kids):

- "child" or "kid" instead of "skater" (unless the context is specifically a skating skill)
- "season" instead of "campaign"
- "practice" instead of "session"
- "registration" instead of "enrollment process" or "signup process"
- "join" or "sign up" instead of "onboard"
- "team" instead of "squad"

## Grammar and style

Active voice over passive.

Short sentences. Aim for an average of 10–14 words.

Avoid stacking commas. If a sentence has more than two, break it into two sentences.

Oxford comma: yes.

Names: "U8," not "u8" or "U-8." "Mites," "Squirts," "Peewee," "Bantams" (capitalized).

First mention: "Lynchburg Youth Hockey Association" or "LYHA." Thereafter: "we" or "us."

"Hill City Coyotes" only when emphasizing brand identity, not in routine operational copy.

## Examples

**Don't (AI-flavored):**
> We're thrilled to welcome you to our vibrant hockey community, a place where young skaters embark on a transformative journey of skill, character, and lifelong friendships.

**Do (LYHA voice):**
> Mites is the youngest division at LYHA. Children learn the game and play for the kid next to them.

---

**Don't (AI-flavored):**
> Our holistic approach ensures every player is empowered to unlock their full potential on and off the ice.

**Do (LYHA voice):**
> Practices focus on skating, puck handling, and team play. Coaches are LYHA staff and Liberty University players. The goal is a real hockey foundation.

---

**Don't (AI-flavored):**
> Whether your skater is lacing up for the very first time or chasing playoff hockey at U14 — we'd love to meet your family!

**Do (LYHA voice):**
> Every age has a place in our program. Reach out and we'll point you to the right division.

## When in doubt

Read the sentence out loud. If it sounds like a brochure, rewrite it. If it sounds like a parent talking to another parent, keep it.
