What to know. No guesswork.

How do I read the bylaws?

Scroll through the PDF above. If you prefer a printed copy, tap Download PDF and open the file on any device.

Can I print the bylaws?

Yes. Download the PDF and print it from your browser or any PDF reader. The file is about 130 KB.

Where can I ask questions?

Email the board at info@lynchburgyouthhockey.com with any questions about governance or bylaw updates.

Questions about the bylaws?

Reach out to the board. We will point you in the right direction.
