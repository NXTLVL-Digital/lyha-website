House Development · Ages 9 to 10

Squirts.
Building the game.

House hockey for ages nine and ten. Children who know how to skate come here to learn how to actually play. Practices are structured, jamborees are competitive, and the goal is a strong foundation before travel begins.

Section 01

Is this right for my child?

Three quick checks. If all three fit, Squirts is the right division.

Age

9 to 10 years old

Squirts covers ages nine and ten at the start of the season. Age eligibility follows USA Hockey birth-year classifications. If you're unsure where your child falls, ask us and we'll confirm.

If your child is 11 or older, look at Peewee.

Skating

Comfortable on the ice

Squirts children should be able to skate without holding the wall, stop, and change direction. Most have played Mites. A child with strong rec skating experience can also be ready.

If your child is just getting started on skates, begin with Learn to Play first.

Format

House hockey, no travel team

Squirts is a house division. Your child plays and practices at LaHaye. Jamborees take the team to nearby rinks a few times a season, but there is no travel team and no away game schedule.

Travel hockey starts at Peewee (ages 11 to 12).

Section 02

What's the commitment?

Squirts is a step up from Mites in structure and competition. No regular game schedule, but jamborees are real competitive hockey.

Season
September through February
Practices
2 per week at LaHaye
Games
0 (jamborees only)
Jamborees
About 3 to 4 per season
Travel
Day trips, within 90 min

A jamboree is a half-day of games at another rink. Teams from several clubs show up and play short games round-robin style. For Squirts, they run from roughly 9 AM to 1 PM. Three or four of these fit into the season. They are more competitive than Mites jamborees. Scores count and kids know it. Travel is day-trip only, mostly within 90 minutes of Lynchburg.

Section 07

Squirts questions.

Specific to this division. The general FAQ covers broader hockey questions.

What's the difference between Squirts and Mites?

Mites is for ages six to eight and puts the focus almost entirely on fundamentals and fun. Squirts picks up where Mites leaves off. Children are older, practices have more structure, and jamborees carry a bit more competitive weight. The format is still house hockey, but the game starts to feel more real.

My child skips straight to Squirts without playing Mites. Is that OK?

It depends on skill level. If your child is nine or ten and already comfortable on the ice, Squirts can be the right starting point. Email us and we'll talk through it. If the fundamentals aren't there yet, we'll tell you honestly.

Does my child need their own gear?

Not to start. We have loaner gear available to borrow. When your child is ready for their own, Play It Again Sports in Roanoke is good for used gear and Pure Hockey in Morrisville (just outside Raleigh) carries brand-new equipment.

Are there tryouts?

No. Squirts is open enrollment. Every child who registers and meets the age requirement gets a spot.

What's the difference between Squirts and Peewee?

Peewee is travel hockey. Real away games, a longer season, and kids playing against teams from other clubs on a regular schedule. That starts at ages 11 to 12. Squirts stays closer to home and focuses on building the skills your child needs to compete at that level.

The Path Forward

After Squirts comes travel.

Peewee is where LYHA kids step into real travel hockey. Games against other clubs, an away schedule, and a season that runs into March. See what that commitment looks like.
