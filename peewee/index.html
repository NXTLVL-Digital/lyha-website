Travel Hockey · Ages 11 to 12

Peewee.
Where travel begins.

Peewee is the first travel division at LYHA. Real games against other clubs, an away schedule, and a season that tests what your child has built. This is the step up from house hockey.

Section 01

Is this right for my child?

Three things to think through before reaching out about Peewee.

Age

11 to 12 years old

Peewee covers ages eleven and twelve at the start of the season. Age eligibility follows USA Hockey birth-year classifications. If you're unsure whether your child qualifies for this season, ask us and we'll confirm.

Ages 9 to 10 belong in Squirts. Ages 13 to 14 belong in Bantams.

Tryouts

There is a tryout

Peewee is not open enrollment. There is a tryout process before the season starts. We hold tryouts in mid-late April. Contact us in the spring and we'll put you on the list for dates and details.

Your child does not need to have played Squirts at LYHA to try out. Kids coming from other clubs or house programs are welcome.

Commitment

Travel games, tournaments, and away weekends

Peewee plays a full schedule of games each season, including tournament weekends, against clubs in the PVAHA region, across Virginia and into neighboring states. Most games are day trips. Some weekends require an overnight stay.

Two practices a week at LaHaye, plus away game weekends. Plan for about six months of scheduling from September through March.

Section 04

What it costs.

Cost should not be the reason a Lynchburg kid can't play.

Peewee tuition covers ice time, coaching, and USA Hockey and PVAHA registration. Travel game costs, including transportation and hotel for overnight trips, are handled separately by families. Current season pricing is included in the registration link we send you after tryouts. We offer financial aid. Ask any board member.

Section 07

Peewee questions.

Specific to this division. The general FAQ covers broader hockey questions.

How do tryouts work?

Tryouts are held at LaHaye Ice Center in mid-late April, typically over one or two sessions. Coaches look at skating skill, puck control, hockey awareness, and attitude. We try to keep it low-pressure. Kids wear a number and skate through drills. No parents on the ice. We send placement decisions within a few days.

What if my child doesn't make the team?

We'll tell you honestly and point you toward the right next step. If your child is close but needs more time at a development level, Squirts is a good fit. If it's a skill gap we can name, we'll tell you what to work on before the next season. We don't leave families without a path.

My child never played at LYHA before. Can they try out?

Yes. We don't require Squirts history at LYHA. Kids coming from other programs, rec leagues, or house teams in other cities are welcome to try out. Bring their USA Hockey registration number if they have one.

How many overnight trips are there?

It varies by season. A typical Peewee season has one to three overnight trips, depending on the schedule and whether the team qualifies for tournaments. We publish the schedule before the season starts so you know what you're signing up for.

Are games on weekends or weeknights?

Away games are almost always on weekends. Practices are weeknight evenings at LaHaye. The exact schedule is set each season, but the pattern holds year to year.
