Section 01

Is this right for my child?

Three things to think through before reaching out about U16.

Age

15 to 16 years old

U16 covers ages fifteen and sixteen at the start of the season. Eligibility follows USA Hockey birth-year classifications. Contact us if you're unsure whether your child qualifies for this season or for Bantams.

Ages 13 to 14 belong in Bantams.

Tryouts

Competitive placement

U16 has tryouts. We hold them in mid-late April. Reach out in the spring to get on the list. Players coming from Bantams at LYHA and from other programs are both welcome to try out.

Coaches evaluate skating, skill, hockey sense, and competitive instinct. We give families direct feedback on placement decisions.

Commitment

The most in the program

Two to three practices a week, a full schedule of games, and overnight travel throughout the fall and winter. This is the top of the LYHA program and the schedule reflects it.

If you want a clearer picture of what a full season looks like, email us before tryouts. We'll walk you through it.

Games are spread across PVAHA, which covers Virginia and neighboring states. Most away games are weekend day trips. Several each season require an overnight stay. Tournament play, if the team qualifies, can mean two to three days away. The full schedule is set before the season opens. Families at this level should plan for a demanding six-month commitment.

What it costs.

Cost should not be the reason a Lynchburg kid can't play.

U16 tuition covers ice time, coaching, and USA Hockey and PVAHA registration. Travel costs, including transportation and hotels for overnight trips, are paid by families separately. Section 07

U16 questions.

Specific to this division. The general FAQ covers broader hockey questions.

How do tryouts work?

Tryouts are held at LaHaye in mid-late April over two sessions. Coaches evaluate skating speed and edge control, puck handling, positional play, and how your child handles competition. Players wear numbers so coaches evaluate without knowing names. Placement decisions come out within a few days of the final session.

Is U16 the highest division at LYHA?

Yes. U16 is the top of the program. Kids who age out of U16 typically move on to high school programs, junior leagues, or other competitive paths. If you want to talk about what comes after, reach out. We're happy to point families toward the right next step.

How much overnight travel should we expect?

More than at Bantams. A typical U16 season includes five or more overnight trips, plus any tournament weekends the team qualifies for. The schedule is published before the season begins. Families at this level should build travel into their calendar from September through March.

Can my child play high school sports and U16 in the same season?

Some kids do. The schedules can conflict, and U16 has a heavy travel load on weekends. Talk to both your school coach and us before committing. We'd rather you know the full picture before tryouts than have schedule conflicts mid-season.

My child never played at LYHA before. Can they try out for U16?

Yes. Players coming from other clubs, other states, or other programs are welcome to try out. Bring their USA Hockey registration number if they have one. Prior LYHA history isn't a requirement.
