Our Mission

Three convictions shape every practice, every game, every season.

No. 01

Develop the Player.

We coach the USA Hockey way: age-appropriate, skill-focused, and built around the love of the game. From a child's first crossover to a Bantam's breakout pass, every drill has a purpose.

No. 02

Build the Person.

Hockey is a vehicle for something larger. We're committed to character, faith, and growing kids into teammates, brothers, sisters, and neighbors who carry themselves well, on the ice and off.

No. 03

Honor the Game.

Sportsmanship is non‑negotiable. Coaches, players, parents and fans abide by USA Hockey's Zero Tolerance policy. We respect the officials, the opponents, and the rink that makes this all possible.

Programs & Pathways

A path for every age and ambition.

Whether your child is lacing up for the very first time or chasing playoff hockey at U14, there's a place for them in our program. Every level is led by experienced LYH coaches and Liberty University players.

01
Learn to Play
No experience required. The first taste of ice for ages 4-8, in a low-pressure environment focused on skating fundamentals and fun.
Ages 4-8 · Beginner
02
House Select
House hockey for Squirts ages 9 to 10. Practices and intersquad games stay at LaHaye, with no travel team and no away game schedule.
Ages 9-10 · House hockey
03
Travel Hockey
Peewee, Bantams, and U16. Practices at home; games across Virginia, the Carolinas, and at times beyond. For families ready to commit to the next level.
U12 · U14 · U16 · Competitive

Warrior Academy Partnership
LYHA does not currently offer a 16+ program. Older players can learn more about Warrior Academy, a Fellowship of Christian Athletes hockey program.

Who We Are

More than hockey players.

Lynchburg Youth Hockey is a nonprofit, parent-run association serving the families of Central Virginia. We're affiliated with USA Hockey, sanctioned 501(c)(3), and proud to call LaHaye Ice Center on the campus of Liberty University our home rink.

"We believe in developing our kids to be more than just hockey players : focused on character development and furthering a relationship with Christ."

That conviction isn't a tagline. It's threaded through how we coach, how we treat officials and opponents, how we raise the bar for what youth sports can be in our community. Every parent, every player, every coach signs a Code of Conduct. And we live by it.

Quick Answers

The questions parents ask first.

How old does my child need to be?

Learn to Play welcomes ages 4-8, but really, if your child is comfortable leaving you at the gate, they're ready to skate.

What equipment do they need?

Full hockey equipment is required. Donated used gear is sometimes available to buy with a donation, but sizes and stock aren't guaranteed. We'll help you figure out the right setup for the right level.

When does the season run?

Travel and House programs follow the USA Hockey calendar, roughly fall through spring. Learn to Play sessions run on shorter cycles year-round.

What does it cost?

Fees vary by program and level. As a nonprofit, we work to keep hockey accessible. Reach out and we'll walk through the numbers honestly.
