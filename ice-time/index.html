Where to Start · No Program Needed

Get on the
ice first.

Before joining a team or signing up for a full program, most kids skate the public sessions at the rink. This page explains where to go, what to expect, and how to get your child on the ice for the first time.

Start in Lynchburg Learn to Play

First choice: LaHaye Ice Center

LaHaye Ice Center is in Lynchburg, right on the campus of Liberty University. It is the home rink for the Lynchburg Coyotes (LYHA) and many Liberty hockey programs.

Check the LaHaye calendar first

Before heading to the rink, check the Liberty Club Sports calendar to see what sessions are scheduled. This is the quickest way to find out what is available right now. The calendar lists Public Skates, Stick and Pucks, and Youth Open Hockey with dates and times. If something is on the calendar, show up and you are good to go.

Public Skate

Public Skate is just for skating. There is no hockey. You can rent skates right at the rink, so you do not need to bring your own. The only thing you need is a skate ticket and warm clothes. Great practice for getting comfortable on the ice.

Stick and Puck

A casual drop-in session. Bring a helmet, a stick, and gloves. Pucks are provided by the rink. Just show up, buy in, and take shots.

Youth Open Hockey

Full hockey gear is required: helmet with cage, gloves, skates, stick, and all protective equipment. Youth Open Hockey is casual drop-in ice time, not structured shifts. Most sessions turn into relaxed games with kids and coaches, and all skill levels are welcome.

Address: 1971 University Blvd, Lynchburg, VA 24515

Phone: (434) 592-3953

Check Lancerlot Sportsplex if LaHaye has nothing scheduled

If there is nothing on LaHaye's calendar right now, the next best place is Lancerlot Sportsplex in Vinton, VA (about 55 miles west of Lynchburg). They list sessions on a weekly basis year-round.

Lancerlot Sportsplex Schedule

Check the Lancerlot ice rink schedule for available sessions. Public Skates, Stick and Puck, and drop-in hockey are typically offered.

Address: 1110 Vinyard Road, Vinton, VA 24179

What to bring

Gear requirements depend on the session. Here is exactly what each one needs:

Public Skate Nothing. No gear at all. You can rent skates from the rink. Just a ticket, warm clothes, and nothing else.
Youth Open Hockey Full hockey gear is required: helmet with cage, gloves, skates, stick, and all protective equipment. All skill levels are welcome. Most sessions are casual games with kids and coaches, not formal shifts.
Stick and Puck Helmet, stick, gloves. Pucks provided by the rink. That is all you need to show up and take shots.
