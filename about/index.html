About LYHA

Built for
Central Virginia families.

Lynchburg Youth Hockey gives kids a place to learn the game, build confidence, and grow inside a steady hockey community.

Section 01 · Mission

More than ice time. A place to belong.

LYHA exists to make youth hockey approachable for families in Lynchburg and the surrounding region.

Learn

Start with confidence.

From Learn to Play through travel divisions, the program gives kids a clear path as they grow on the ice.

Compete

Play with purpose.

Players learn effort, teamwork, and responsibility through practices, games, and tournament weekends.

Belong

Build community.

Families, coaches, and volunteers keep the Coyotes moving season after season.

Home Ice

LaHaye Ice Center is where the season starts.

Practices and home games are held at LaHaye Ice Center on Liberty University's campus, giving local families a consistent home rink in Lynchburg.
