Section 01 · Our Board

The people helping move the season forward.

Meet the six board volunteers who guide the LYHA season.

President
Brandon Brown

Brandon is married to Dara Brown and has two boys; Parker and Dawson. He is the owner of the local company Brown's heating, air and plumbing. Brandon has always enjoyed helping his boys in any sport they play. He has served as the president of LYHA since 2022 and is looking forward to growing the program in Lynchburg.
