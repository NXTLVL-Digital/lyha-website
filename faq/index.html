Common Questions

FAQ.
Where to start.

For questions about a specific division, check that division's page. These cover the whole program.

Section 01 · Getting Started

New to hockey. Where do we start?

We've never done hockey before. Where do we start?

If your child is between four and eight and has never been on skates, start with Learn to Play. It teaches skating from scratch. If they can already skate unassisted, Mites is the next step. From there, kids move up by age: Squirts, Peewee, Bantams, and U16.

Does my child need to know how to skate?

It depends on the program. Learn to Play teaches skating from the first lesson. Every other division assumes your child can move on the ice without holding the wall. They don't have to be polished. They need to be on their feet.

How young can kids start?

Learn to Play accepts children as young as four. Most kids who start that early spend one or two seasons there before moving into Mites.

Are the programs coed?

Yes. All LYHA programs are open to all kids regardless of gender.

Do we have to live in Lynchburg?

No. We have families from surrounding counties. If you can get to LaHaye Ice Center, you're in range.

Section 02 · Programs & Placement

Finding the right program.

How do I know which division is right for my child?

Age at the start of the season is the primary factor. Learn to Play: 4 to 8. Mites: 6 to 8. Squirts: 9 to 10. Peewee: 11 to 12. Bantams: 13 to 14. U16: 14 to 15.

If your child is on a border year, email us and we'll help you decide.

What's the difference between house hockey and travel hockey?

House hockey stays local. Learn to Play, Mites, and Squirts practice at LaHaye and play jamborees at nearby rinks. The focus is development.

Travel hockey plays real games against other clubs across the region. Peewee, Bantams, and U16 have away weekends and occasional overnight trips. The schedule is heavier and the commitment is bigger.

My child played for a different club last year. Can they join LYHA?

Yes. We welcome transfers. Bring your USA Hockey registration number. For travel divisions, the standard tryout process applies.

Can my child play two divisions at once?

No. Players are rostered on one team per season.

Section 03 · Costs & Financial Aid

What does it cost?

How much does a season cost?

Pricing varies by division. We publish current season fees with the registration link we send you. Email us and we'll tell you what this season looks like.

What does tuition cover?

Ice time, coaching, and USA Hockey and PVAHA registration. It does not cover personal gear or travel costs for away games and overnight trips.

Is financial aid available?

Yes. Cost should not be the reason a Lynchburg kid can't play. Ask any board member. The conversation is confidential.

Are there additional fees during the season?

House divisions pay jamboree fees as the schedule firms up. Travel divisions pay their own gas and hotel costs for away weekends. These are paid by families and vary by season.

Section 04 · Gear & Equipment

Getting the right gear.

What gear does a child need?

Helmet with cage, neck guard, shoulder pads, elbow pads, hockey pants, shin guards, skates, gloves, and a stick. The jersey is covered by registration. A full gear list comes with your welcome email.

Do we need gear before the first practice?

Not if you use our loaner program. We have used equipment available to borrow, enough to get a child through a first season. Ask when you register. First come, first served.

Where do we buy gear near Lynchburg?

For used gear, Play It Again Sports in Roanoke is about an hour away. For new gear, Pure Hockey in Morrisville, NC is about three hours, just outside Raleigh. Both are linked on each division page.

What size gear does my child need?

The staff at Pure Hockey can fit your child properly. For used gear, bring the child to Play It Again so they can try on skates in person.

Section 05 · Season & Scheduling

How the season works.

When does the season start and end?

House divisions (Learn to Play, Mites, Squirts) run September through February. Travel divisions (Peewee, Bantams, U16) run September through March.

Where do practices and home games happen?

LaHaye Ice Center on the campus of Liberty University, 1971 University Blvd, Lynchburg, VA. Directions and parking are on the LaHaye website.

What is LaHaye Ice Center like?

A full-size NHL rink with a heated lobby and locker rooms. Liberty University operates it through Club Sports. More about the facility.

How do I get the season schedule?

Schedules are sent to families once finalized each August. Travel schedules are published before the season begins. Once our calendar page is live, all club events will be posted there.
